`liberators/desktop.sh` lines 139 to 150 set a dozen GNOME keys with literal values written into the liberator: `org.gnome.mutter overlay-key` to empty, the `org.gnome.desktop.wm.keybindings` switchers to Alt-Tab and Shift-Alt-Tab, `panel-main-menu` to none, and the `media-keys` screenreader and magnifier bindings to none. Two things are wrong with that block.

- The keys and values are Molt's, not the user's. Every other preference a sleeve carries comes from Molt-matts and renders per host; these cannot be changed, added to or dropped without editing the framework, and a second user of Molt gets Matt's GNOME bindings.
- Every `gsettings set` ends `2>/dev/null || true`, so a key that no longer exists, a schema that is not installed, or a value GNOME refuses is silent, and `molt upgrade` says the sleeve is ready over a binding that did not take (the No Silent Errors shape; `check` cannot see the gap either, since it reads back only `overlay-key` and the favourites).

Fix, when it is picked up: the key/value pairs move into the user stack, as a `[desktop]` table or a `config/desktop/gsettings.toml` beside the favourites file, read with `molt_liberator_field` or the same TOML reader; the liberator applies what the stack declares, reads each value back and names any that did not take, and `check` compares all of them, not one. Carried from the vc board's small items on 22 Oct 2026; low, since kovacs is the only GNOME sleeve and its bindings are right.
