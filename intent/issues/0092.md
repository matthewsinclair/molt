## What

On 4 Nov 2026 the 1.4.3 cut stopped at its candidate's CI. Run 41898191402, the push of `candidate/v1.4.3` at 4e0714a, passed Test on Ubuntu and Shell Script Analysis. Its Test on macOS job (126813241516) asked for `macos-latest` and was never given a runner: it has no runner name and no steps, and GitHub cancelled it after 15 minutes (19:34:11Z to 19:49:14Z) with "The job was not acquired by Runner of type hosted even after multiple attempts", beside the notice "Due to capacity constraints, jobs targeting macOS arm64 runners may experience longer queue times." A candidate requires the macOS leg, so Test Summary failed the run, and the cut stopped before it tagged.

Pplr's `macos-latest` jobs were stranded the same way twice that day while Cldr's and Ccusage's `macos-15` jobs got runners, and Pplr pinned `macos-15` (Pplr 8a1760f). hv's ruling (b), relayed by molt-vc on 4 Nov: Molt's `test-macos` pins `macos-15` too.

## The change

- `.github/workflows/tests.yml`: `test-macos` runs on `macos-15`, where it ran on `macos-latest`. The Linux jobs stay on `ubuntu-latest`, and `pr-checks.yml` has no macOS job.
- `.github/workflows/README.md`: the job table's runner for `test-macos`.
- `CHANGELOG.md`: a new `[1.4.4] - unreleased` section. It opens by saying Homebrew goes from 1.4.2 to 1.4.4, because 1.4.3's cut stopped before its tag and devbin never re-dates a dated note. Its Changed carries this line.

`macos-15` is an arm64 image as well, so the pin does not take Molt out of a squeeze on arm64 capacity. What it changes is the pool: on 4 Nov the `macos-15` pool served Cldr's and Ccusage's jobs while the `macos-latest` pool served neither Pplr's nor Molt's. It also stops Molt's macOS changing whenever GitHub moves the label, so Molt tests on the macOS that Pplr, Cldr and Ccusage test on.
