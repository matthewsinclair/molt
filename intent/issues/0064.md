## What happens (found by molt-dc, 25 Oct 2026, running 0062's suite under an empty HOME; confirmed by vc)

molt.bats "molt doctor runs without crashing, and exits as its checks went" fails under a HOME where the declared font cannot be seen. It fails on main at 60398ee too. In lib/molt.sh, doctor's font check (line 2122) calls `molt_font_available "$font_family"` as a bare statement, then `case $? in`. bin/molt and lib/molt.sh run `set -euo pipefail`. So when the font is missing (exit 1) or cannot be measured (exit 2), errexit ends doctor right there: it prints nothing about the font, gives no verdict, and exits 1. That is exactly the case the check exists to report, and the reason is never shown (No Silent Errors). A fresh sleeve without its font hits this for real, and so does the kovacs release check under its throwaway HOME.

## Spec (vc, 25 Oct 2026)

- Capture the exit status without tripping errexit: `local font_rc=0; molt_font_available "$font_family" || font_rc=$?`, then `case $font_rc in`. Each of 0, 1 and 2 prints its line, and doctor carries on to its verdict.
- Search lib/ and liberators/ for the same shape, a bare call followed by `$?`, under errexit. Fix each one the same way, or name it in the issue's as-built note as checked.
- Tests: doctor with the font absent (a fake fc-list listing other families) prints the NOT installed line and the rest of the checks. With fc-list absent, it prints the cannot-measure line. Both are red with the change reverted.
- The full suite runs under Homebrew bash and /bin/bash, once under an empty HOME as well, plus the critic leg.
