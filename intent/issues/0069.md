`molt doctor`'s GitHub auth check (`lib/molt.sh`, the `timeout 5 ssh -T git@github.com` line) reports "not authenticated" when the ssh handshake takes longer than five seconds, even though authentication works. It reads timing out the same as being refused.

kovacs-sysadm found this on 26 Oct 2026. From kovacs the handshake took about 6 s, so doctor counted the check as not passed on every run. Meanwhile ssh answered "Hi matthewsinclair!" and gh was logged in. The same check had passed that morning, so it depends on the route and its latency, not on the sleeve's setup.

To fix it, give ssh its own connect bound instead of a short outer kill: eg `ssh -o BatchMode=yes -o ConnectTimeout=10 -T git@github.com`, under an outer timeout long enough to finish an authenticated handshake. A timeout must report as unreachable ("could not reach github.com within N s"), not "not authenticated", so the verdict says what was checked. Tests cover three cases: a stub ssh that answers late but within the bound passes; one that never answers reports the timeout, not "not authenticated"; one that answers "Permission denied" reports not authenticated.
