`liberators/keys.sh` runs four commands as root with nothing checking them: `sudo make install` inside the keyd build subshell (`keys.sh:50`), `sudo systemctl enable keyd` and `sudo systemctl start keyd` (`keys.sh:57-58`), and `sudo keyd reload` (`keys.sh:74`). Molt runs under `set -euo pipefail`, so a failure of any of them aborts the liberator rather than passing silently, but it aborts with the command's own output and no line of Molt's naming what was being done or what to do, unlike every fault the liberator reports through `molt_error`. critic-shell raised it on issue 0033's change (cc, 22 Oct 2026), where it was out of scope: 0033 replaced the file writes with `molt_sudo_install`/`molt_sudo_remove` and left the commands alone.

Each should run through a check that names it on failure, in the liberator's own words: what was being enabled, started, installed or reloaded, and that the liberator stops there. No behaviour change on a sleeve where they succeed. gyges and kovacs are the sleeves that run keys today; nothing on rhadamanth.
