brew_maintain (liberators/brew.sh) runs `brew update`, `brew upgrade`, `brew upgrade --cask` and `brew cleanup -s` without checking any of them, and ends with molt_info. A failed upgrade returns 0, so `molt maintain` reports brew maintained over the failure.

Fix: each of update, upgrade, upgrade --cask and cleanup that fails fails the hook, naming the step. brew doctor and brew missing stay advisory. Test with a fake brew that fails each step in turn.

Found by ac's 24 Oct doc review (code notes); vc routed it to dc.
